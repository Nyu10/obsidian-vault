- **Voluntary:** _"Not reluctantly or under compulsion"_ (2 Cor 9:7).
- **Proportional:** _"In keeping with your income"_ (1 Cor 16:2).
- **Sacrificial:** _"They gave according to their means, and beyond their means"_ (2 Cor 8:3).

how could a New Testament Christian—who has seen the full grace of God in Christ dying on the cross—give **less** than an Old Testament farmer?


**No Unfair Burden:** Giving should not result in the giver becoming destitute while the receiver lives in leisure. The goal is relief of distress, not a reversal of misfortune where the donor ends up suffering.


_"As for the rich in this present age, charge them not to be haughty, nor to set their hopes on the uncertainty of riches, but on God, who richly provides us with everything to enjoy. They are to do good, to be rich in good works, to be generous and ready to share."_
