### Prayer

*   **Prayer For Others:** *Myself, Family, Friends, Work, Life Group


- **He'd ask what your heart is chasing.** Jesus talked about money more than almost any other topic—not because money is evil, but because it easily becomes a master. He warned that _"where your treasure is, there your heart will be also"_ (Matthew 6:21). He'd likely gently ask what you were really hoping that options trade would give you—security, validation, an escape—and remind you that a screen can't give you peace.
    
- **"Go, and leave your life of sin" (John 8:11).** Grace in the Gospels is never "it doesn't matter, keep doing it." It's _"I don't condemn you—now walk away from the thing that's hurting you."_ Repentance isn't beating yourself up for weeks; it's closing the door on the habit and moving forward differently.
    
- **Stewardship over gambling.** In the Parable of the Talents, Jesus praised steady, faithful stewardship—building something real over time rather than risking everything on a gamble or burying your head in the sand out of fear.
